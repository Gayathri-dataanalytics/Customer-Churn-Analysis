 Customer Churn Analysis

 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn to understand why customers leave and identify high-risk customer segments.
The analysis was performed using Excel, Python, SQL, and Power BI.

Business Problem

The main objective is to identify:

- Which customer segments have the highest churn?
- Which contract types are most likely to churn?
- How do internet service and payment methods affect churn?
- Does customer tenure influence churn?
- Which customers are at higher risk of leaving?

Tools & Technologies

- Excel – Data cleaning and basic analysis
- Python – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- SQL – Business analysis and customer segmentation
- Power BI – Interactive dashboard and visualization

 Dataset

- Total Customers: 7,043
- Total Features: 21
- Churned Customers: 1,869
- Overall Churn Rate: 26.54%

 Key Insights

- Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate at 42.71%.
- Fiber optic customers have a churn rate of 41.89%.
- Electronic check users have the highest churn rate at 45.29%.
- Customers with 0–12 months tenure have the highest churn rate at 47.44%.
- Customers with high monthly charges show higher churn.
- Customers without tech support have a churn rate of 41.64%.
- Month-to-month customers using electronic check have a particularly high churn rate of 53.73%.

  Business Recommendation

The analysis suggests focusing retention efforts on month-to-month customers, new customers, electronic check users, fiber optic customers, and customers with higher monthly charges.
Offering better onboarding, flexible retention offers, improved support, and targeted customer engagement may help reduce churn.
